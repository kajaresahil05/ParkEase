20. ENTRY LOGIC
==========================================================
Entry IR detects a waiting vehicle.
Entry IR alone MUST NOT automatically open the gate.
User:
1. Logs in
2. Books slot
3. Vehicle reaches Entry IR
4. User taps ENTER PARKING
5. Backend verifies authorization

6. ESP32 receives authorization
7. Servo opens
8. Vehicle crosses gate
9. Exit IR detects vehicle
10. ESP32 reports entry crossing
11. Gate closes
12. Slot sensor detects parked vehicle
13. Slot becomes OCCUPIED
==========================================================
21. EXIT LOGIC
==========================================================
Exit IR detects vehicle leaving.
1. Exit sequence begins.
2. Gate opens.
3. Occupied slot that becomes empty is identified.
4. Slot becomes EXITING.
5. EXITING remains unavailable.
6. Entry IR confirms vehicle has completely exited.
7. Gate closes.
8. EXITING → AVAILABLE.
With only five sensors, exact source slot cannot always be determined from Exit alone.
For this prototype, identify the occupied slot whose sensor changes OCCUPIED → EMPTY during the active exit sequence.
